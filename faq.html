Current positions

Since 2022 the path into an Italian university runs through fixed-term research posts, then a six-year tenure track, then a permanent professorship.

The usual sequence
  1. Incarico di ricerca, with a master's degree
  2. Incarico post-doc or contratto di ricerca, with a PhD
  3. RTT, the tenure track
  4. Professore associato, then professore ordinario

Incarico di ricerca research assignment

An entry-level post for recent graduates who want to try research, usually on a funded project. It is a collaboration contract, not employment.

Who can apply
Holders of a laurea magistrale (or equivalent master's) obtained no more than 6 years before the deadline. No PhD needed.
Length
1 to 3 years, at most 3 years in total per person.
Contract
Parasubordinato (collaboration), exempt from income tax, with INPS contributions. Minimum pay set by ministerial decree.
Not open to
Permanent university staff, past RTT or RTD holders, and students enrolled in a degree or PhD.
Legal basis
Art. 22-ter, L. 240/2010, added by DL 45/2025.

Incarico post-doc post-doctoral position

A short post-doc for early-career researchers, focused on research with limited teaching.

Who can apply
PhD holders (or medical specialists) by the deadline. No minimum or maximum time since the PhD. Public research bodies may accept a strong CV in place of the PhD.
Length
1 to 3 years, at most 3 years in total per person.
Contract
Fixed-term employment. Pay at least that of a starting part-time university researcher.
Not open to
Permanent university staff, past RTT or RTD holders, and enrolled PhD students.
Legal basis
Art. 22-bis, L. 240/2010, added by DL 45/2025.

Contratto di ricerca research contract

A longer post-doctoral job tied to a specific research project, the main replacement for the old assegno di ricerca.

Who can apply
PhD holders (or medical specialists). Some universities also admit final-year PhD students who finish within 6 months.
Length
2 years, renewable for 2 more; up to 1 extra year on funded projects. At most 5 years in total per person.
Contract
Fixed-term employment, paid between a part-time and a full-time university researcher's salary.
Not open to
Permanent university staff and past RTT or RTD holders.
Legal basis
Art. 22, L. 240/2010, as rewritten by DL 36/2022.

RTT tenure-track researcher

The Italian tenure track. A six-year post with research and teaching duties; with the ASN and a positive evaluation, it converts into a permanent associate professorship.

Who can apply
PhD holders (or medical specialists). No minimum experience. Foreign PhDs need Italian recognition (equivalenza), which can be requested by the deadline and must arrive before hiring.
Reserved calls
Some calls (art. 24 c. 1-bis) are reserved for candidates with at least 36 months of PhD or research at institutions other than the hiring one. Time abroad counts.
Length
6 years, not renewable. Can be shortened for candidates with long prior contracts.
Leads to
Professore associato, from the third year on, with the ASN and a positive evaluation.
Not open to
Current or former permanent professors and researchers, and anyone who already spent 3 years as RTT.
Legal basis
Art. 24, L. 240/2010, as rewritten by DL 36/2022.

Professore associato associate professor

A permanent professorship, the second of the two professor ranks ("seconda fascia").

Who can apply
Holders of the ASN for the sector at associate or full level, current associate or full professors, or scholars holding an equivalent post abroad, per the ministry's correspondence tables.
Length
Permanent.
Legal basis
Art. 18, L. 240/2010. Universities can also hire directly scholars with 3 or more years in an equivalent post abroad (chiamata diretta); those hires don't appear on bandi.mur.gov.it.

Professore ordinario full professor

The highest academic rank ("prima fascia"), permanent.

Who can apply
Holders of the ASN for the sector at full level, current full professors, or scholars holding an equivalent full professorship abroad.
Length
Permanent.
Legal basis
Art. 18, L. 240/2010.

Tecnologo research technologist

A research-support post: running labs, infrastructure, data or project management, rather than leading research.

Who can apply
Set by each call, typically a degree plus relevant experience.
Contract
Fixed-term employment; length set by each call.
Legal basis
Art. 24-bis, L. 240/2010.

Legacy positions

DL 36/2022 abolished these for new hires. You'll still see them in CVs, in older calls, and in a few calls finishing under transitional rules. Past time in them can matter for new applications.

Assegno di ricerca research grant

The standard Italian post-doc before 2022: a research grant, not an employment contract, with limited social security.

Replaced by
Contratti di ricerca (2022), then also incarichi post-doc and di ricerca (2025).
Length
1 to 3 years, renewable, up to 6 years in total.
Today
No new calls. Whether past assegni count toward the new 11-year cap is not settled.

RTDa junior fixed-term researcher, type A

A fixed-term researcher post with no route to tenure, often funded by projects. A PNRR-funded variant ran during the recovery-plan years.

Replaced by
RTT (2022).
Length
3 years, extendable by 2.
Today
Only stray calls remain. Having held an RTDa excludes you from the new pre-tenure contracts, but not from RTT.

RTDb senior fixed-term researcher, type B

The pre-2022 tenure track: a three-year post that became an associate professorship with the ASN. It required prior experience, such as a completed RTDa or 3 years of assegni.

Replaced by
RTT (2022).
Length
3 years, not renewable.
Today
No new calls. Its experience requirement survives, loosely, in reserved RTT calls.

Terms and rules

What are GSD, SSD and the area codes?

Italian academia sorts every post into a discipline. Since 2024 the main unit is the GSD (gruppo scientifico-disciplinare), e.g. 13/ECON-04 Economia applicata. Each GSD sits in one of 14 broad areas (13 is economics and statistics) and contains narrower SSDs (settori scientifico-disciplinari), e.g. ECON-04/A. Older calls use the pre-2024 settori concorsuali, such as 13/A1.

What is the ASN?

The Abilitazione Scientifica Nazionale is a national qualification, granted per sector and per rank (associate or full), that makes you eligible for professor calls. It is a qualification, not a job. Candidates trained abroad can apply for it too.

Is there a limit on how long I can stay on fixed-term contracts?

Yes. Each type has its own cap (3 years for incarichi, 5 for contratti), and the total across contratti, incarichi, RTT and RTD posts is 11 years, at any Italian institution. Parental and sick leave don't count. Grants such as borse di ricerca, and posts abroad, are not on the list. Universities may read these rules differently, so check with the university if you're close to a limit.

I have a PhD from outside Italy. Can I apply?

Yes, to every position type. For incarichi and contratti, the selection committee judges whether your PhD is equivalent. For RTT, you need formal Italian recognition (equivalenza): request it before the call deadline, since it must arrive before you can be hired, and it can take months.

Do I need to be an Italian or EU citizen, or speak Italian?

No position type requires Italian or EU citizenship. Non-EU researchers get a research permit through a hosting agreement filed by the university, which needs about 90 days. Italian is not required by law, but some calls ask for it or hold the interview in Italian.

Where does the data come from?

From bandi.mur.gov.it, the ministry's portal where universities and research bodies publish their calls, scraped daily. Eligibility is checked only against national rules, never against individual calls, and kinship exclusions are not checked.
